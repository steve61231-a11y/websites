What's wired up

Lenis smooth scroll

Buttery, inertia-based scrolling synced to GSAP's ticker for frame-perfect timing.

ScrollTrigger reveals

Fade, stagger, and pin animations that fire as elements enter the viewport.

Cursor tracking

An eased custom cursor that scales and morphs over interactive elements.

Magnetic buttons

Interactive elements pull toward the pointer within a small radius.

Parallax depth

Layered elements move at different speeds via a simple data-speed attribute.

Reduced-motion aware

Respects prefers-reduced-motion and disables the custom cursor on touch devices.
